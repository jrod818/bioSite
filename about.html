Growing Up in Napa

Childhood Photo

Kelsey grew up in Napa, California. Throughout her childhood, she had opportunities to travel and participate in several sports. These experiences helped develop her work ethic and curiosity about the world around her.

To learn more about the area where Kelsey grew up, visit Visit Napa Valley .

Sports were an important part of her childhood. She participated in basketball, volleyball, and track and field, with basketball being the sport she played for the most years.

Experiences

Travel also played an important role in Kelsey's life. Some memorable experiences included visiting New York and Costa Rica. In New York, she saw Mary Poppins and visited the 9/11 Memorial. In Costa Rica, some of her experiences included horseback riding and visiting a mud bath.

Accomplishments

One of Kelsey's proudest accomplishments is becoming a business owner. Her experiences with sports and travel helped develop qualities such as independence, curiosity, and a strong work ethic.
